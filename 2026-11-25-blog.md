# The sun will still rise on Saturday

Reading between the lines of the messaging falling out of the Epsom Key National Party into the right commentariat:

The right will not be giving up power to a left coalition with a slight majority without resistance:

Some of their levers may be

a) a party vote recount.
b) the dissolution of parliament and a snap election.
c) a right minority government holding power.

If Opportunity has the numbers to be king maker then expect a right wave of pressure to have them bow down into a supply and confidence agreement with some policy and extra cabinet portfolios.

If Opportunity go left and the majority is slim - then the question becomes can Chippy hold the line?

The final card that Opportunity could put down is the call for a snap election: but they’ve just got through the gates of parliament, and the risk of a snap election is the National Party vote disappearing as the overhang genie is out of the bottle.

Does the left have a plan for a left overhang strategy?

The clock is ticking fast to Friday.

As my learned colleague said to me at lunch.

“It's okay Joe, the sun will still rise on Saturday.”
