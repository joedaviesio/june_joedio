Saturday 6 – Sunday 7 February 2027

Hanmer Springs Ski Area, New Zealand

A night of music and art up in the mountains behind Hanmer Springs. Live music and DJs, good people, a lot of sky.

We have the ski lodges for the night, so you will have a bed. Head up on Saturday, back down on Sunday.

Getting up there

What to bring

  • 2 lunches and snacks for yourself
  • Sleeping bag and pillow, plus whatever you need for the night
  • Running trainers or tramping footwear
  • Warm layers and a rain jacket, it gets cold up there
  • Sunscreen
  • 1.5 litres of water
  • A backpack to carry it all, we walk up
  • A small gift for the secret gifts. Everyone gets someone to give to and gets one back
  • BYO alcohol
